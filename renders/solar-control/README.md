# Valley solar-control image studies

These bitmap studies were created with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool after
their corresponding fixed-camera geometry was generated from
[`models/design.json`](../../models/design.json). The 3D model remains the
authority for dimensions, openings, fins, furniture count and site clearances.

## Assets

- `option-a-deep-roof-visor.png` — passive 1.60 m roof projection study.
- `option-b-floating-brise-soleil.png` — open horizontal blade-canopy study.
- `option-c-upper-gable-veil.png` — initial upper-only operable-fin study.
- `selected-option-c-permeable-patio.png` — selected Rev 09 development image,
  including the 1.25 m visor and permeable patio with exactly two compact chairs
  and one coffee table.

## Selected-image prompt record

The selected image used the actual valley-gable site photograph, the
model-generated Option C fixed-camera view and the initial Option C atmosphere
study as references. ImageGen was instructed to preserve the 17.0 × 7.5 m barn,
continuous Belgian brick, black tiled gable roof, neighbouring brick building,
hedge and narrow paved passage; confine nine slender matte-black operable fins
to the triangular glazing above the 3.0 m transom; keep all rectangular
ground-floor glass completely clear; show a 1.25 m roof visor; and add a compact
water-permeable open-jointed local-stone patio with exactly two small outdoor
chairs and one low coffee table. It was also instructed not to add a canopy,
pergola, extra furniture, walls, doors, windows, plumbing or altered landscaping.

This is a geometry-review photoreal concept, not approved construction imagery.
Final fin angles, controls, glazing and any concealed retractable screen require
PHPP/designPH, thermal-bridge, wind, drainage and structural design.
