# Hybrid Flemish Brick + Black Lantern

Status: **photoreal concept study, not a construction proposal**

This alternative keeps the same **17.0 × 7.5 m** barn envelope and continuous
ICF wall shell as the full Black Barn concept. The exterior finish changes with
the plan:

- the front double-height living volume is matte-black vertical fibre cement;
- the rear bedroom/service volume is contemporary warm Flemish brick veneer;
- deep black aluminium or pressed-metal window liners express the thick ICF wall;
- one crisp black flashing marks the material transition;
- black roof, rainwater goods and opening frames unify both volumes;
- glass remains clear and unobstructed;
- the tractor lane, pasture gate, hedges, neighbour, cobbles and mature trees stay.

Fibre cement is the rainscreen finish, not the principal insulation. Thermal
performance must come from the selected ICF assembly, roof build-up, airtightness,
glazing and coordinated junctions. The dark facade also requires a ventilated
cavity and project-specific summer-comfort modelling.

## Generation method

Generated with Codex's built-in ImageGen image-edit mode. Each prompt used the
full-black concept as the geometry target, the matching property photograph as
site truth, and the Brick Veil image only as a brick-material reference.
