# Black Barn Lantern — ICF concept set

This four-view set replaces the developed exterior direction with a matte-black
fibre-cement rainscreen over a concept insulated-concrete-form wall shell. It is
photoreal pre-design material, not a measured ICF, structural or facade design.

## Fixed design logic

- One barn volume: 17.0 m long × 7.5 m wide.
- Eaves: 3.2 m; ridge: 5.2 m; symmetrical roof pitch: approximately 28.1°.
- Concept external wall build-up: approximately 35–45 cm, subject to the selected
  ICF system, engineer, thermal model, facade subframe and finishes.
- Exterior: ventilated matte-black vertical fibre-cement rainscreen and a
  near-black standing-seam roof; no brick veneer.
- Glazing: large clear openings with deep ICF reveals and no screens, fins,
  louvers or other visual obstructions.
- Front: double-height living, dining and kitchen behind the full glass gable.
- Middle: transition and stair.
- Rear: bedrooms, bathroom/service rooms and recessed courtyard entrance.
- Optional loft: partial open mezzanine above only the rear 7–8.5 m.
