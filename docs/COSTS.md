# Cost Assumptions

- Status: **dated pre-design estimate, not a quotation**
- Estimate basis: carried forward from the August 2026 concept board

None of the figures below have been independently refreshed for this document.
Market pricing, tax treatment and eligibility must be confirmed with contractors,
the project architect, quantity surveyor, accountant and notary before relying on
them.

## Estimate assumptions

- 127.5 m² ground-floor footprint.
- Full demolition of the target barn assumed, subject to structural report.
- Residential version includes a high/mid-range fit-out, services and glazing.
- Professional fees are excluded from the construction subtotal.
- The optional loft, stair and rooflights are **not included** in the base range.
- The current ICF shell, continuous Belgian brick veneer and black ceramic-tile
  roof were **not separately priced** in the carried-forward board estimate;
  replace the shell and roof allowances with coordinated supplier/contractor
  quotes.
- The custom smoked-oak entrance portal, concealed entrance light and shielded
  exterior building-lighting scheme were not separately priced.
- Site access, neighbour protection, temporary works and unknown ground conditions
  may materially change the estimate.

The rental scenario used an unverified indicative rent of €1,100–1,400/month for
a rural two-bedroom property of this quality.

## VAT assumptions

The board assumed:

- 6% demolition-and-rebuild VAT for an eligible sole and own home at or below
  200 m²;
- 6% for an eligible private rental used as a principal residence for at least
  15 years;
- 21% for non-residential use or when the reduced regime does not apply.

These are dated legal/tax assumptions, not advice. Confirm the current rules,
definitions, documentation and project eligibility before procurement.

## Professional fees

The board allowed the following on top of construction:

- architect and stability engineer: approximately 8–12% of works;
- EPB reporter and safety coordinator: approximately €4,000–6,000.

Also budget separately for survey, soil investigation, planning costs, utility
changes, façade/glazing engineering, interior design, landscape repair,
contingency, finance and insurance as applicable.

## Optional loft exclusion

The partial loft requires a separate allowance for:

- structural floor and acoustic build-up;
- stair, guarding and joinery;
- rooflights and blinds;
- the wide triangular rear-gable window, reinforced opening and black perimeter
  framing;
- fire-safety measures;
- lighting, electrical and ventilation changes;
- additional design and engineering.

Allow separately in the main-envelope design for the custom full-height oak door,
vertical timber recess lining, deep black portal, long pull handle, flush drained
threshold, concealed entrance light, eaves downlights, wiring, drivers and lighting
controls.

Price it only after the internal roof profile and usable area are measured.
