Visual concept · Schorisse, Belgium

The Glass-Gable Barn

A restrained Flemish barn outside. A warm, open living hall framing the valley inside.

  • 17.0 × 7.5 m envelope
  • Valley-facing glass gable
  • Open rear mezzanine
01 · Exterior

A contemporary barn, grounded in its place.

The architecture changes. The working lane, neighbouring building, hedges, trees and cobbled courtyard do not.

The glass gable

The living room opens toward the valley while the narrow paved passage and neighbouring brick building remain exactly where they are.

The shared lane

Bedroom windows sit toward the rear, the stair marks the transition, and the larger living opening faces the valley end—without narrowing tractor access.

The courtyard arrival

A timber-lined oak entrance portal and discreet rooflights sit behind the established hedges and hydrangeas. The cobbles and mature canopy remain the foreground.

02 · Living hall

The valley becomes the room.

The front half stays open to the ridge, bringing kitchen, dining and living together around the real Schorisse outlook.

03 · Open loft

Extra living space, without losing the volume.

A compact lounge and library sits above the rear master and service core. The front living hall remains completely double-height.

04 · Selected direction

Belgian Brick Lantern.

One continuous brick exterior over the thick ICF shell, a locally familiar black tiled roof, deep black frames, restrained building lighting and a welcoming oak entrance portal.

05 · Conceptual floor plans

A comfortable home, not just a zoning diagram.

The revised plans account for the thick wall shell, privacy, real door operation, wardrobe and service storage, kitchen clearances and a stair that reaches the mezzanine at the ridge.

  • 1 ground-floor master bedroom
  • Italian walk-in shower
  • Separate toilet + sink
  • Open kitchen + living hall
  • Open mezzanine · no plumbing

This website is the visual concept presentation. Dimensions, structural thinking, feasibility and cost assumptions are maintained separately as project documents.
